Who we are

A manufacturer with practical roots and a forward view.

Steel Experts India manufactures premium-quality Mild Steel Hexagon Bars, Flat Bars, Bright Bars, Carbon Steel Round Bars, Alloy Steel Bars, HB Wire, HHB Wire and customized steel products.

Our team brings hands-on production understanding to every enquiry. We study the intended application, align the process to the requirement and keep communication direct from quotation through dispatch.

  • GST registered since 2022
  • Manufacturer-direct supply
  • 26–50 skilled employees
