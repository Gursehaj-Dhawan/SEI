Half Round Steel Bar

Special half-round profiles manufactured for close fit, aesthetic detail and custom applications.

Product overview

Manufactured around your specification

Half round steel bars reduce the need to machine a full round section when the application needs one flat face and one curved face. They can support guides, trim, fabricated parts and special engineering designs.
